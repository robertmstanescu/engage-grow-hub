FAQ

Not sure which one you need?

Which pillar should I start with if I'm not sure?

−

If people are missing what you tell them: Internal Communications.

If people are leaving, or barely arriving in the first place: Employee Experience.

If every process only runs because you personally remember it: People Operations.

If the decision in front of you is too sensitive to delegate and too rare to hire for: Fractional HRBP.

If pay gets set case by case and you now need a structure you can explain out loud: Pay Transparency.

If your newest managers are learning the job by guessing: Manager Enablement.

What if my problem spans more than one pillar?

−

Plenty of real problems do. Internal comms and employee experience overlap all the time, and people operations underpins both. Pay transparency and manager enablement travel together too: your managers are the ones who answer the pay questions.

Book the free call and describe the symptom rather than the pillar you think it belongs to. We'll tell you where to start.
