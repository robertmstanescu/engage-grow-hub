Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 Apr 2026

1. Who we are. The Magic Coffin (the “Company”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us at hello@themagiccoffin.com.

2. What data we collect. We collect data you give us directly (e.g. your name, email, company, message) when you fill in our contact or subscription forms, and limited technical data (IP address, device, browser) collected automatically for analytics and security.

3. Why we use it (legal bases under the GDPR). We process your data to (a) respond to your enquiry: necessary for steps prior to entering a contract; (b) send marketing emails: only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time; (c) measure traffic and improve our services: based on our legitimate interest in running the site, balanced against your privacy.

4. Cookies. We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies and, with your consent, analytics cookies. You can manage or revoke consent at any time from the cookie banner.

5. Sharing. We share data only with processors that help us run the service (hosting, email delivery, analytics). All processors are bound by data-processing agreements and process data inside the EU/EEA or under approved transfer mechanisms.

6. Retention. We keep contact submissions for as long as needed to handle your request and up to 24 months thereafter. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics logs are kept for 14 months.

7. Your rights. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or port your data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these rights write to hello@themagiccoffin.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Security. We use TLS in transit, encryption at rest, role-based access controls and regular backups. No system is 100% secure, but we follow industry best practice.

9. Changes. We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

The Magic Coffin's privacy policy explains what personal data this website collects through its contact and subscription forms, why it collects it, how long it keeps it and who processes it, under the GDPR. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@themagiccoffin.com.
