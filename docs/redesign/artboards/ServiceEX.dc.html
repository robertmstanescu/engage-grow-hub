WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE

Nobody walks that hallway alone by accident.

A good employee experience runs deeper than a wellness Friday. It is a series of moments, onboarding, a first difficult month, a promotion that should have happened sooner, each one designed rather than left to whoever happens to be free that week. Design the moments and the retention numbers tend to sort themselves out.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got a different question? That's what the free consultation is for.

How is this different from just running an engagement survey ourselves?

−

Plenty of engagement survey tools measure and stop there, short of diagnosing or fixing anything.

The Autopsy tells you where the experience breaks and why, inside your own organisation, rather than a benchmark score against companies you've barely heard of.

Will employees know their interview answers are attributed to them?

−

Individual sentiment stays anonymised by default in every written deliverable, kept untraceable to any single name.

People tend to say more when they trust that.

What if we don't have exit-interview data to analyse?

−

That strengthens the case for starting one, and the Autopsy notes the gap itself as a finding.

The audit draws on whatever data exists, plus direct interviews, either way.

Does this replace or work alongside our existing engagement survey tool?

−

Alongside, usually.

The Séance adds diagnosis and a real action loop on top of whatever tool sends the survey. Sending the survey was always the easy part; acting on what it finds is where the real work sits.

What's a vampire moment in an employee's journey, concretely?

−

The onboarding programme that fades out after week one.

The engagement survey that produces a number, goes into a slide and gets filed away for good.

Small, undramatic moments that add up to someone checking out by month three.

How long does onboarding redesign take?

−

The Awakening runs four to six weeks.

It covers the full journey from offer acceptance to a settled, contributing team member: content, manager guides, a buddy programme and an LMS structure where you use one.

Where the managers doing the onboarding are new to managing themselves, Manager Enablement adds a full manager kit, small practice cohorts and one-to-one coaching.

Do you build an employee journey map?

−

Yes. The Autopsy delivers a one-page map of your full employee lifecycle, and the Awakening designs the onboarding stretch of it in detail.

Each one is mapped against what happens inside your organisation, built fresh each time rather than pulled from a generic template.
