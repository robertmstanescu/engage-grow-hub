THE PROBLEM

A restructure or a difficult exit lands on whoever's most senior in the room, moving fast, with everyone learning the plan as it happens. The moment passes quickly, and it only gets one chance to go well. A restructure teaches the whole company how it will be treated when things get hard. That lesson outlives the restructure.

THE FIX

A background built for this call

Hard announcements and crisis communications across 36 countries, with roughly 500 people supported along the way. That is the judgement a fractional HRBP brings into the room: someone who knows what makes a hard conversation go right.

Someone who understands the employee behind the process

A social sciences background (anthropology, sociology, media and communication) built around reading how people experience a hard moment, alongside the legal and procedural side of it. Confidential, fast to start and squarely on your side.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got a different question? That's what the free consultation is for.

Is this a replacement for our employment lawyer?

−

This sits alongside legal advice, as a complement to it rather than a substitute.

Legal counsel tells you what's compliant. A fractional HRBP tells you how it's likely to land with the people affected, and helps you say it well.

Plenty of hard moments call for both.

How fast can you start once we call?

−

For The Vigil, situation assessment happens within 48 hours of kickoff.

This pillar is built around the reality that these situations are rarely convenient to wait on.

Is everything discussed confidential, including from other stakeholders in the business?

−

Yes. Confidentiality is the load-bearing term of this entire pillar.

We sign a mutual NDA before the situation assessment begins, ahead of anything else.

What happens if the situation escalates beyond what was originally scoped?

−

We re-scope it in the open, straight away, rather than folding it into the original quote.

You'll know as soon as the shape of the work changes, with the option to adjust or stop.

What is a fractional HRBP, exactly?

−

A senior HR Business Partner who works with you part time or per engagement instead of full time.

The judgement of a senior hire on restructures, employee relations and comp decisions, at a fraction of the cost and commitment.

How is this priced, day rate or retainer?

−

Both exist.

The Vigil is a single hard-moment engagement, scoped and priced per situation.

The Resurrection is a monthly retainer with a three-month minimum, for ongoing strategic partnership rather than one fire to put out.

What's the difference between a fractional CPO and a fractional HRBP?

−

A fractional CPO usually owns the whole People function and strategy end to end, often across several clients at once.

A fractional HRBP, this pillar, brings judgement to specific, high-stakes calls: a restructure, a difficult exit, a comp decision that sets precedent, while the function itself stays owned in-house.

Plenty of companies need the second long before they can justify or find the first.

Can you also train our managers or sort out our pay structure?

−

Yes, as separate services. Manager Enablement trains a whole group of new managers with a kit, a cohort and coaching. Pay Transparency builds job levels, pay bands and the pay conversation for the EU Pay Transparency Directive.

Fractional HRBP covers the individual calls in between, and the three work well together.
