THE PROBLEM

A new manager gets the title, the team and the calendar in a single afternoon. The training usually arrives months later, while the team is already deciding whether to stay. In the UK, 82% of managers got the job with zero formal management training, and people with an ineffective manager are more than twice as likely to plan to leave (CMI, 2023).

THE FIX

A background built for this problem

Years of writing manager briefings for hard announcements across 36 countries, and training branch staff in banking before that. The kit comes from what managers asked for in the moment, when the news was hard and the clock was running.

Someone who understands how people learn

A social sciences background (anthropology, sociology, media and communication) means the programme works with how people learn from each other, through practice and peers, alongside the slides.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got a different question? That's what the free consultation is for.

How is this different from a leadership course?

−

Manager Enablement is built from your company's own policies, tools and moments.

Managers practise the conversations they'll have next week, with colleagues who share the context, and leave with a kit written for your company.

How many managers do we need for a Coven?

−

Four to twelve works best.

With fewer managers, The Familiar gives the same practice one to one, and The Grimoire works at any size.

Is it online or in person?

−

Online by default, with in-person sessions where the trip earns its place.

How do you measure whether it worked?

−

Agreed at the start: an upward-feedback pulse from each manager's team before and after, plus staff turnover and absence over the following six months.

Can it cover a specific change, such as a restructure or a pay review?

−

Yes. Sessions and scripts can be built around the change coming up, so managers rehearse the exact conversations they're about to have.

It pairs well with Internal Communications and Pay Transparency work.

Is this coaching for senior leaders too?

−

The focus is first-time and newer managers.

For senior leaders facing a sensitive decision, Fractional HRBP brings a senior partner into the room.

What is manager enablement?

−

Manager enablement gives new managers the tools, practice and support for the people side of the job: one-to-ones, feedback, passing on company news and hard conversations.

At The Magic Coffin it runs as a written kit (The Grimoire), an eight-week cohort (The Coven), one-to-one coaching (The Familiar) and a monthly clinic (The Night School).
