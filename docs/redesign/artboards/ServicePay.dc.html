FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got a different question? That's what the free consultation is for.

Does the EU Pay Transparency Directive apply to small companies?

−

Yes, most of it. Pay ranges before the interview, the ban on pay-history questions, visible pay criteria and the right to request pay information apply to employers of every size.

Countries can exempt companies under 50 people from sharing pay progression criteria, and gender pay-gap reporting starts at 100 employees.

When do the rules apply in my country?

−

The EU gave member states until 7 June 2026 to turn the Pay Transparency Directive into national law, and most countries missed that deadline. Each one is writing its own version on its own timetable.

The Autopsy includes a dated, country-by-country summary for everywhere you hire, checked again before anything goes live.

We're in the UK or Sweden. Is this relevant?

−

The UK sits outside the Directive, and Sweden has asked the EU to renegotiate it.

Clear pay still helps you hire and keep people, and it gets you ready early if the rules arrive. We'll tell you plainly which parts matter for you.

Is this legal advice?

−

It sits alongside legal advice. We build the structure, the words and the rollout; your employment lawyer signs off the local legal detail.

If you need a lawyer, we'll introduce one.

Do we need pay-benchmarking software?

−

It helps, and it's your choice. The Unmasking works from your own pay data plus a benchmark you pick: a paid tool, public salary surveys or recruiter data.

The method is written down either way, so you can explain every band.

What if the check finds a pay gap?

−

Then you found it first, which is the best place to be.

The Unmasking separates gaps your criteria explain from gaps that need closing, and gives you a plan and a budget for closing them over one or more pay cycles.

Is Pay Transparency the same as People Operations?

−

It's a focused slice of it. People Operations builds the systems and paperwork across the whole employee lifecycle. Pay Transparency covers one job end to end: levels, bands, the pay conversation and the rules behind them.

You can start here and move into People Operations later, or run both side by side.
